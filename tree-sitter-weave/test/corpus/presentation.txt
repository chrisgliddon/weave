==================

    background: "art/ferry_dusk.png"
}

stage mara {
    image: "art/mara_neutral.png"
    name: "Mara"
}

=== start ===
@scene ferry_dusk
@show mara
@speaker mara
The ferry leaves at dusk.
@hide mara
@speaker
After she leaves.
-> END
